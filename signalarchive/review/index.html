SIGNAL ARCHIVE — Demo Video for App Review

Submission ID: 0e377958-edf9-4dd5-90c7-ca2fb2be0f68  |  Version 1.0 (2)
Recorded on a physical iPhone 16 Pro Max (iOS 26.6.1) and a paired Apple Watch SE 3 — not a simulator.

There is no designated hardware. This app does not pair with, connect to, or require any external accessory. Core Bluetooth is used in scan-only mode to count how many Bluetooth advertisements are detectable nearby; that count becomes an ambient "signal density" value in the game. The app never calls connect(), never exchanges data, and never stores peripheral names or identifiers.

1. iPhone — full workflow

  1. 0:00 — Location permission primer, then the system prompt. Granted.
  2. 0:18 — HOME on the physical device. RESONANCE DETECTED, research at 0%.
  3. 0:20 — SCAN tab. The Bluetooth primer states plainly: “counts only the number of nearby Bluetooth signals; device names and identifiers are never displayed or stored.” Then the system prompt. Granted — note that no pairing sheet appears, because there is nothing to pair with.
  4. 0:33 — A 15-second scan. Watch the rows SIGNAL_9ACA, SIGNAL_17B6, SIGNAL_2B15… accumulate. These are anonymous counters for advertisements detected in the room, not connections. Nothing is paired, read, or written.
  5. 0:51 — An artifact fragment is found (R-001) and analyzed. Its form and record are restored to 30%.
  6. 0:56 — The archive grid (1 / 125) and the artifact detail screen, including the DATA tab.
  7. 1:28 — MENU → CONTINUE RESEARCH → the in-app purchase sheet. One non-consumable product, priced in the storefront of the signed-in account.

2. Apple Watch — optional companion

Filmed with a camera so that the physical Apple Watch itself is visible, as requested.

  • 0:03 — The companion app on the physical Apple Watch, already in sync with the iPhone (SCAN READY, research 32%, the latest artifact listed).
  • 0:10 — The three scan bands (WIDE / FOCUS / DEEP) with their odds meters.
  • 0:18 — The Bluetooth permission prompt on the Watch. Again, no pairing sheet — the Watch scans for ambient advertisements only.
  • 0:26 → 0:34 — 15 SIGNALS rising to 19 SIGNALS during the scan. This is the clearest illustration of what the Bluetooth permission is for: the app is counting, not connecting.

The Apple Watch is paired to the iPhone by the user through the standard Watch app; our app performs no pairing of its own and adds no accessory.

The Apple Watch is optional. The iPhone app is complete without it.

Reaching the paid content

Completing Layer 0 normally takes several days of walking, so there is a permanent shortcut: MENU tab → “CONTINUE RESEARCH”. It is always visible regardless of progress and leads straight to the purchase sheet. There is one non-consumable product only.

The first three scans after a fresh install always yield a fragment, so the whole scan → discover → analyze loop can be verified indoors, standing still.

Permissions

  • Location (When In Use) — resolves an approximately 153 m sector. Coordinates are converted to a sector symbol and discarded immediately; no history is kept.
  • Bluetooth — counts nearby advertisements only, as described above.
  • Access WiFi Information — reads only the currently joined network name, for the “home base” feature. It is hashed on device and never leaves it.

All three are optional. With any of them denied, scanning, analysis and the archive all continue to work. There is no sign-in and no account, so no demo account is needed.
