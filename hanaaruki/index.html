歩いた歩数の累計で、植物が12段階の姿を見せていく観察アプリ。歩数は「ヘルスケア」から読むだけで、書き込みはしません。
An iOS observation app in which your cumulative step count carries one plant through twelve stages. It only reads your step count from Health and never writes to it.
