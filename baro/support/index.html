Support & FAQ

Questions, bugs and feature requests are welcome at akiaki1984@gmail.com.

The score won't load

BARO needs location access and a network connection for its first forecast. Check Settings → BARO → Location, then pull down on the Today screen to refresh. If a forecast was downloaded earlier, BARO keeps showing it and labels it with when it was updated.

What do LOW / MODERATE / HIGH mean?

They are bands of the 0–100 Body Weather Risk: 0–34 LOW, 35–64 MODERATE, 65–100 HIGH. The score is built from how much and how fast barometric pressure is changing, plus humidity and temperature shifts. It is an app-defined weather-sensitivity indicator, not a medical probability.

My alerts aren't arriving

Turn notifications on in Settings → BARO → Notifications, and enable "High risk alerts" inside the app. Alerts are scheduled on your iPhone from the forecast BARO already downloaded — iOS decides when background refreshes run, so opening the app occasionally keeps the schedule current.

When does personalization start?

After at least 10 check-ins that include at least 5 "good" days and 5 symptomatic days, spread across different weather. Logging good days matters as much as logging bad ones — without both, there is no pattern to learn.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

Settings → BARO Pro → Restore Purchases, with the Apple ID used for the purchase. BARO Pro is a one-time purchase tied to that Apple ID.

The widget is stale

Widgets read the score BARO already calculated, so they refresh when the app does. Open BARO once to refresh, and note that iOS also limits how often widgets can redraw.

Can I switch to inHg or °F?

Yes — Settings → Units. Pressure and temperature units are independent of the app language.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete All Local Data. This removes every check-in, the cached forecast and the widget snapshot. It cannot be undone.
