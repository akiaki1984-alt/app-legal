Lamptap Support

Support page for the iOS app “Lamptap”.

FAQ

Are my photos uploaded?
No. Lighting and shape estimation run on your iPhone.

What’s the difference between Glow and Relight?
Glow lays soft light over the photo and saves for free. Relight reads faces and shapes to create lit and shadowed sides. Previews are free; saving Relight is a one-time purchase.

New iPhone?
Tap “Restore Purchases” in Settings on an iPhone signed in with the same Apple ID.

“Can’t Save to Photos”
Go to Settings > Lamptap > Photos and choose “Add Photos Only”, or use “Save to Files”.
