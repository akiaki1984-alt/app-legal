Lamptap Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Lamptap (“the App”) lets you place light anywhere in a photo and adds shading. The App does not collect personal information and never sends your photos or edits to the developer or third parties. No account is required.

1. Information we collect

None. The App uses no advertising, analytics or tracking.

2. Photo access

You choose photos with the system photo picker; only the photo you pick is passed to the App. The App does not ask to read your whole library. Saving uses add-only access and never changes your original.

3. On-device processing

Lighting and shape (depth) estimation run entirely on your iPhone with an on-device machine-learning model. Photos are never uploaded by the App. Saved photos may sync to iCloud depending on your Photos settings.

4. What the App stores

To let you resume editing, the App keeps a copy of the photo you picked and your edit settings in its private storage on the device. They are excluded from iCloud Backup, can be deleted any time from “Edits”, and are removed when you delete the App.

5. Location

Location (GPS) data is removed from saved photos by default, unless you turn on “Keep Location” in Settings.

6. Purchases

The one-time Relight purchase and restores are handled by Apple’s App Store. The App never receives your payment details.
