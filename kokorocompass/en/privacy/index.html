Privacy Policy

App: Photo Stars
Formerly: Kokoro Compass
Provider: Kazunari Aoki
Last updated: November 16, 2026

Photo Stars turns each photo that has a location into one star, drawing a night sky that is yours alone. The number of stars equals the number of photos with a location. Reading your photos and drawing the sky both happen entirely on your device; your photos and location are never sent to our servers. No account is required.

1. Data we do not collect

  • Information that identifies you (name, email, phone number)
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA), ad tracking, or ads
  • Usage data from analytics tools (such as Firebase or Google Analytics)
  • Any transmission of your photos, location, or activity

The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.

2. Reading your photo library

To draw your sky, the app asks for access to your photo library. It reads only:

  • The capture location (latitude and longitude) and capture date of your photos (videos are not included)
  • Whether a photo is marked as a Favorite in Photos (to make its star slightly brighter)
  • The image and thumbnail shown when you tap a star

Photos without a capture location are not read and do not become stars. If you allow "Selected Photos" only, the app reads only the photos you chose. All reading happens on your device; photos and their locations are never sent anywhere. The app never changes or deletes the photos in your library.

If you use iCloud Photos and a photo's original is not on your device, iOS may download it from Apple's iCloud to display it. That transfer is performed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

3. Your current location

The app asks for "While Using the App" location permission the first time you tap the compass button. Only if you allow it, your location is used to show where you are on the sky and for compass mode (re-centering the stars on your location and turning the sky with your phone). The heading sensor is used only while compass mode is on.

Your location is used only on your device; it is never stored or sent anywhere. The app does not get your location when you are not using it. You can change the permission anytime in iOS Settings. The sky works without it.

4. Data stored on your device

The following is stored only on your iPhone:

  • Which photos you made "special stars" (their identifiers in your photo library; the photos themselves are not copied)
  • Images of your sky for the Home Screen widget and Apple Watch (with Pro). These contain only star dots: no photos, text, place names, or coordinates.

Deleting the app removes this data from your device. It may be included in device backups (such as iCloud Backup), which Apple handles under its policy.

5. Data from the previous version (Kokoro Compass)

Places, attached photos, and visit records you saved in the previous version remain on your device after updating. They are not shown in the new version, and the new version does not save places.

If you had turned on "iCloud Backup" in the previous version, that old data continues to sync with your own iCloud (Apple CloudKit private database). We cannot access it. The new version has no setting to change this. To remove the old data, delete the app; data left in iCloud can be deleted from iCloud storage management in iOS Settings.

6. Home Screen widget and Apple Watch

Both are Pro features. The app draws an image of your sky and places it in an App Group (a shared space for apps on the same device). The widget shows that image; the Lock Screen widget shows your star count.

For Apple Watch, the app sends only the sky image and the star count from your iPhone to your paired Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity. Nothing passes through our servers. If Pro is no longer active, the images are not placed or are removed.

7. Exporting your sky

With Pro, you can export the sky you are viewing as an image without text or guides. The image is handed to the iOS share sheet, and you choose where to save or share it. Services you share to handle it under their own policies.

8. Notifications, maps, and place names

The new version does not use notifications and does not ask for notification permission. Any "notify me when nearby" alerts you set up in the previous version stop the first time you open the new version. The app does not display maps, and it does not look up place names from your photos' locations.

9. In-app purchases

Purchasing and restoring Pro (one-time purchase) are handled by Apple StoreKit. We never receive your Apple ID or payment details; Apple handles them under its privacy policy.

10. Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. As noted, it does not collect personal information at all.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Important changes will be announced in the app or on this page.
