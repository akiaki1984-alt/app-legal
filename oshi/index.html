Oshi

推しの写真と次の予定を、ホーム画面のウィジェットに常駐させるアプリ。写真も予定も端末の中だけで扱い、外部へは送信しません。

A widget app that keeps a photo of your favourite and the next date you are looking forward to on your home screen. Photos and dates stay on your device and are never transmitted.
